How it works

Forest, terrain, soil and regional weather inform the score. Data coverage varies by location and species.

The model is experimental. Its weights have not been validated against independent observations. Missing inputs are omitted; scores are not encounter probabilities.

National overview: 500–1,000 m. Local detail: 50 m. Finer cells do not mean finer weather measurements.
